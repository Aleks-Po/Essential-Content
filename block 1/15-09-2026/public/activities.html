Activities and Events

Private Lessons

During private sessions, the trainer will focus on you personally and select a training program best suited to your needs.

Group Lessons

Group classes allow you to be part of a team with other students, perform group exercises, support one another, and meet new people.

Water Games

Anyone interested is welcome to join us for our water games. Your group can choose the type of game and fully customize it to suit your team.
